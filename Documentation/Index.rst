NewsBunny
=========

----

NewsBunny is a backend module for `EXT:news <https://extensions.typo3.org/extension/news>`__
that gives editors a dedicated working area for the news records of a site. It
combines a filterable record list, control panels for the most common record
operations and a listing of the pages the records are stored on.
